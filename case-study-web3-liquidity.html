Case Study · Web3 Liquidity

Turning institutional research into continuous market commentary

A Web3 liquidity firm continuously produced high-value research. OM Digital built the workflow that connected published thinking, approved market sources, and institutional distribution needs into continuous market commentary.

Book a 20-minute walkthrough

The Challenge

Research that kept coming. Meaningful engagement that didn’t.

The firm’s research team operated at a high, consistent cadence, publishing rigorous market analysis and methodology papers for a narrow, highly specific audience. The intellectual output was continuous and credible.

What was missing was the production layer between that output and the engagement it was capable of generating. Commentary on live market conditions required time the team didn’t have. The research cycle and the engagement cycle were running at different speeds.

The Solution

A system anchored to the firm’s research, not the news cycle.

OM Digital built a research-to-market communication workflow that connected the firm’s published thinking, approved market sources, and agreed narrative rules.

The workflow curated relevant developments, matched them against the firm’s research themes, and produced review-ready commentary in the firm’s voice. Outputs remained under client editorial control before publication.

How It Works

Four stages, running continuously.

01

Research and narrative ingestion

The workflow absorbs the firm’s published research, positioning, methodology, terminology, and approved themes.

02

Approved-source market curation

Relevant developments are selected from agreed sources and filtered against the firm’s market focus.

03

Research-to-market synthesis

Market events are connected back to the firm’s views, research, and institutional audience needs.

04

Review-ready output with client approval

Outputs are drafted in the firm’s voice and prepared for review before publication.

What Changed

Continuous research output. Continuous client engagement.

The firm gained a repeatable way to keep research active after publication, connect its views to current market conditions, and support institutional conversations with timely commentary.

01

Engagement that matches the research

Commentary connects the firm’s published thinking to live market conditions, on the same cadence the research is produced.

02

Research that keeps generating value

Published theses continue producing relevant output as markets evolve around them. Nothing goes quiet after publication.

03

Voice and credibility maintained

Every output reflects the firm’s intellectual framing and terminology. Institutional positioning is consistent across every touchpoint.

04

Reduced production burden

The research team stayed focused on the thinking. The workflow handled curation, synthesis, formatting, and distribution preparation.
