FAQs

What does OM Digital build?

OM Digital builds AI-assisted workflow systems for financial firms. These systems turn market intelligence, research, founder expertise, and brand context into controlled, on-brand communication across chosen channels.

Who is this for?

Crypto funds, fintech platforms, trading businesses, and financial infrastructure companies that need more consistent market communication without building a large internal production team.

Is this fully automated?

It can be, but it does not have to be. Workflows can be configured as draft-only, review-first, or approved publishing depending on the client's control requirements.

How do you reduce AI risk?

Workflows are built around approved sources, defined brand rules, structured prompts, review gates, and client editorial control.

What channels can the workflow support?

LinkedIn, newsletters, blogs, client updates, sales notes, community channels, internal briefs, audio, and other formats agreed during scoping.

What happens in the 20-minute walkthrough?

We review your current communication workflow, identify where research or market intelligence gets stuck, and show how an AI-assisted workflow could support your team.
