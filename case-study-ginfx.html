The Growth Challenge

GinFX operates in one of the most competitive areas of fintech: retail trading. The product gave users mobile-first access to forex and CFD markets. The brand combined trading, education, Web3 positioning, and community engagement.

But the marketing operation needed to move at the same speed as the market. Market events were creating daily opportunities to educate traders, start conversations, reinforce product value, and bring users back to the platform. Without a scalable system, those opportunities were difficult to capture consistently.

Before The System

  • Manual content production
  • Market news arriving daily
  • Limited team bandwidth
  • Missed community moments

What OM Digital Built

OM Digital built an always-on content and community engine for GinFX. The system combined market intelligence, AI-powered content workflows, automated publishing, community campaigns, and performance optimisation. Market developments were monitored and filtered for relevance. High-signal topics were converted into educational content, social posts, community prompts, and product-aligned messaging. Content memory helped reduce repetition, while theme management kept the brand narrative consistent across channels. The result was a growth operation that could move with the market without requiring GinFX to build a large internal marketing team.

The System in Action

Every day, the system monitored relevant market developments and filtered them for GinFX’s audience. High-signal topics were converted into educational content, social commentary, community prompts, and product-aligned messaging. Publishing infrastructure then moved approved content into the right channels, supporting Telegram growth, social activity, and trader engagement.

The Results

In 90 days, GinFX’s Telegram community grew from 1,350 to 35,000 members. Community engagement increased approximately 30x. Monthly active users increased approximately 12x. Trading volume continued to grow month over month, with average monthly trading volume per user also increasing. This showed that the system was not only increasing audience size. It was helping GinFX attract and activate higher-quality users.
